# PLC Program Versions

## Overview

The PLC program for the Nebico system went through **10+ generations** during development and field commissioning.

Rather than being developed as a single fixed program, the control logic was progressively revised as new requirements, commissioning observations, and field conditions were encountered.

The versions therefore represent the **development and refinement of the control program**, with later generations building upon the behaviour and lessons of the earlier ones.

---

## Version Development

The general progression was:

```text
Initial Program
      ↓
Testing & Debugging
      ↓
Field Implementation
      ↓
Observed Issues
      ↓
Logic / Architecture Changes
      ↓
New Program Generation
      ↓
Field Testing
      ↓
Further Refinement
